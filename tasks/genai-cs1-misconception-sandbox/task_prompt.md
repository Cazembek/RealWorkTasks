# Task Prompt

You are the newly hired **Digital Learning Experience Project Manager** at the Center
for Teaching Innovation (CTI) at Ashgrove University. Your director, Dr. Elena Vasquez,
has asked you to produce a **Project Design & Pilot Plan** for a new tool: a low-stakes,
GenAI-assisted "debugging sandbox" for CS1 students, aimed at reducing reliance on
AI-generated fixes that students don't understand.

You have been given five source materials, attached below. Use them as your evidence
base — do not invent misconception statistics, survey results, or prior-tool findings
that aren't in the packet, and do not contradict anything stated in them.

1. `01_directors_memo.md` — the commissioning memo, including hard constraints on
   budget, timeline, staffing, vendor, integration approach, and policy compliance.
2. `02_misconception_research_brief.md` — internally coded frequency data on 8 CS1
   misconception categories.
3. `03_prior_tools_audit.md` — a heuristic-evaluation audit of two prior AI debugging
   tools and why each failed.
4. `04_student_survey_results.md` — quantitative and qualitative student survey data
   on AI tool use, low-stakes practice, and gamification.
5. `05_ux_heuristics_reference.md` — Nielsen's 10 usability heuristics, provided as a
   reference list only.

## Your deliverable

Write a single Project Design & Pilot Plan document, using exactly these nine section
headings, in this order:

1. **Executive Summary** (200 words or fewer)
2. **Problem Statement & Misconception Targeting**
3. **Lessons from Prior Tools**
4. **Tool Concept & UX Design**
5. **GenAI Guardrail Design**
6. **Project Plan** (timeline, roles, budget)
7. **Risk Register**
8. **Evaluation & Success Metrics**
9. **Accessibility, Equity & Privacy**

## Requirements

- The plan must be internally consistent and must satisfy **every** hard constraint in
  the director's memo simultaneously — budget, timeline, staffing, vendor, integration
  approach, and policy. Do not propose working around a constraint (e.g., requesting
  more budget, a different vendor, or a new LTI integration); design within them.
- Ground your misconception targeting and tool design explicitly in the data provided
  — cite the specific categories, frequencies, prior-tool failures, and survey findings
  you're drawing on.
- When you apply a UX heuristic, name it and tie it to a specific, concrete design
  decision — don't just assert the tool will be "intuitive" or "user-friendly."
- The GenAI guardrail design must directly address the specific over-reliance and
  engagement-collapse failure modes documented in the prior-tools audit.
- Budget must be itemized and must not exceed the stated cap. Timeline must fit the
  stated build and pilot windows exactly.
- Write as a real deliverable meant to be read and approved by the Associate Dean —
  not as an essay about the topic in general.

Produce the complete document now.
